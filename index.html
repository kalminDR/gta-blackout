What we are actually measuring

A launch day is easy to describe and hard to measure. Everyone will guess how much work went undone on 19 November. We would rather count it, and we would rather start counting before it happens instead of finding the pattern afterwards.

So we take a reading every hour, from sources anyone can check, and we publish the raw readings as we go. By November there will be eleven weeks of ordinary Thursdays to compare against. That baseline, not the launch day itself, is the hard part.

  • AttentionTwitch viewers across the hundred largest live streams, by game and by language
  • PlayConcurrent players on Steam, and where the Grand Theft Auto titles sit in its live ranking
  • WorkPublic activity on Hacker News, as a rough pulse of people who are supposed to be working
  • MovementCongestion on major commuter roads in six cities
  • LoadPlayStation Network and Xbox Live service health
  • AnticipationViews and subscribers on Rockstar's own channel

Every reading ever taken is committed to a public repository with a timestamp, which means none of it can be quietly adjusted later. Read the code and the raw data.
