What we measure and why

We track attention, play, movement, service health, resale prices and prediction-market odds. Nine public sources are read every hour. The launch is compared with New York transit and Wikipedia baselines since 2023.

New York's subway carries about 4.5 million people on an ordinary November Thursday; a drop of roughly 90,000 would be unmistakable.

Some results are measured directly. Nobody publishes daily data on who took the day off, so that figure is estimated. We tested GitHub and Stack Overflow as productivity measures, rejected both, and publish the evidence.

Get the results

Two emails, then silence. In October, we publish the forecast before GTA VI arrives. In late November, we send the results. The first matters because a prediction made in advance cannot be rewritten to flatter the outcome.

Grand Theft Auto VI can make people plan months ahead, coordinate with friends and change their routines without managers, deadlines or reminders. Some of those people will not finish a mandatory thirty-minute course. [eureka], which builds serious games and gamified training, runs the project. The problem is not that a game earns attention. It is that most training does not.
